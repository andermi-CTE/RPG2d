Epoch: The Arkavian Tower

Start · Play Epoch · Beginner guide

FVGD teacher project · revision r002

A small Dragon Warrior–style NES RPG starter with original placeholder graphics and enemies. This is a classroom foundation for your own art, story and expansion.

Start the demo

  1. Extract the entire ZIP first. Keep the folders together.
  2. Open Play Epoch in Chrome or Edge. No server or internet is required.
  3. Walk with WASD/arrows. Approach a road and press E.
  4. Explore the countryside. Press E beside the northeast tower to face its sentinel.
  5. Win, then return to town. The completion banner confirms the goal.

Fight with 1; heal with 2; flee an ordinary encounter with 3. Escape returns to town outside combat. R restarts the countryside position, preserving RPG stats.

Included

Town, four roads into one countryside map, tile movement, turn-based battles, herbs, inn, shop, experience, levels, gold, defeat/recovery, tower encounter and optional local stat saving.

Project information and next steps. Town artwork remains the class blockout. Tower interior, equipment, spells, multiple regions, music and final sprites are future assignments.
